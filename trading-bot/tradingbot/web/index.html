Demo / paper only. Fills are simulated from free price feeds (Binance, Yahoo), with estimated spreads, commissions and financing. No real money, no broker orders. Research so far shows no strategy reliably passes the acceptance gates across all markets (see the Research tab). Past results do not guarantee future results.

Runs the paper trader over a recent window: parameters are fitted only on the year before each 90-day window, then all selected markets trade through one account with the shared risk rules — the same engine as live paper trading.

Forward test on live prices. Each tick fetches the latest closed bars, re-fits parameters every 90 days, and simulates fills. The first tick only initialises the account; trades start from the next bar.

Backtests one market over all cached history. Walk-forward re-fits on a trailing window and trades only unseen data (honest); fixed uses default parameters over everything (in-sample, optimistic).

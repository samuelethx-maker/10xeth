The ritual

Ninety minutes,
three rounds.

There is no right way to do it, but most people settle into the same rhythm: get hot, get cold, sit still for a while. Here is how a typical session divides up.

Arrive HeatRest HeatRest HeatRest Dry
0 min306090
  • Sauna, 8–15 min
  • Sea, as long as feels right
  • Rest on the deck

  1. Arrive

    Check in at the hatch ten minutes before your slot. Change in the cabin, leave bags in the dry lockers, rinse at the deck shower.

  2. Heat

    Climb to the upper bench for more heat, stay low for less. Water goes on the stones every few minutes — ask first, it's a shared room.

  3. Sea

    Down the ladder, between the yellow buoys. In winter the water is around 9°C, so a dip of thirty seconds is plenty.

  4. Rest

    Sit on the deck in a towel and let your heart rate settle. Water and a flask of nettle tea are always out.

First time

What to bring, and what to expect.

  • Swimwear and a towelSwimwear is required in shared sessions. A second towel for the deck is a luxury you won't regret.
  • Something for your feetThe pontoon is non-slip but cold in winter. Sliders or neoprene socks work well.
  • A warm layer for afterwardsA big jumper and a hat. You will feel warm for longer than you expect, then suddenly not.
  • A refillable bottleThere's a drinking-water tap on the deck. Drink more than you think you need.

House rules

  1. Swim only between the yellow buoys, and never alone — tell someone when you go in.
  2. No glass, no alcohol, nobody who has been drinking.
  3. Ask before adding water to the stones.
  4. Phones stay in the lockers. The cabin is a quiet, photo-free room.
  5. When the harbour flag is red, sea dips are off. The sauna stays open and the deck shower is cold enough.

If you're pregnant, have a heart condition or high or low blood pressure, check with your doctor before using a sauna or cold water. Our hosts are trained in first aid and water rescue, but they can't give medical advice.

Getting here

  • On foot or by bike: bike racks at the top of the slipway.
  • By bus: stop B on Harbour Road.
  • By car: the boatyard car park is for boat owners only; use the pay-and-display on Quay Street.
  • Access: the slipway is steep and the pontoon moves with the tide. Call ahead if you need help getting down — we'll meet you at the top.

Questions

Before you come.

Do I have to go in the sea?

No. Plenty of people use the cold deck shower instead, or just sit outside between rounds. The sea is there if you want it.

What happens in bad weather?

Rain, wind and cold make no difference — arguably they help. If a storm warning makes the pontoon unsafe, we'll cancel and refund your session in full, and tell you by email at least two hours before.

Can I cancel or move my booking?

Yes, up to 24 hours before your session, for a full refund or a free move to another slot. Inside 24 hours we can't refund, but you're welcome to send someone in your place.

Is it suitable for children?

Children aged 12 to 15 can join shared sessions with a paying adult. For younger children, book a private hire, where the adults in your group are responsible for them in the cabin and on the deck.

Is the cabin mixed?

Yes, all sessions are mixed and swimwear is required in shared sessions. If you'd prefer a women-only or men-only session, a private hire is the way to do it for now.
